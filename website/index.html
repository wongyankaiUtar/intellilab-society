What we do

Why we're different

We fixed what the other club sites got wrong

Our team reviewed four existing AI club and AI education websites. Every gap we found became a feature here.

Fixed

Content that updates itself

Those sites relied on someone remembering to update them, so information went stale — one even had a countdown frozen at 00:00:00. Our AI News page refreshes itself every time you open it.

Fixed

You can actually sign up

Most offered a single email address and nothing else. We have validated registration and contact forms that keep your draft as you type.

Fixed

Real people, real credibility

One club site had no About page or committee at all. Ours introduces every officer, our history, and how to reach us.

Fixed

Search and filter everywhere

Finding a specific resource meant scrolling forever. Workshops, projects, gallery and news are all filterable and searchable.

Fixed

Genuinely responsive

One reviewed site misaligned badly at other resolutions. Ours is built mobile-first on a fluid grid and tested from 360px upward.

Fixed

Social, not siloed

None of them embedded social media. Our Contact page carries a live social feed and one-tap sharing on every article.

For the curious

How this site works

We are an AI club, so it would be odd to hide the engineering. Here is what runs underneath — and what we do with your data, which is very little.

Cookies

Your appearance and name

Your light/dark choice, the name you gave us and a visit counter are stored in cookies with a 30-day expiry. Cookies are the only one of the three that survives closing the browser entirely.

localStorage

Your shortlist and account

Bookmarked projects and workshops, plus your member account if you created one, live in localStorage. It persists indefinitely on this device until you clear it.

sessionStorage

Your unfinished forms

Draft registration and contact forms use sessionStorage, which the browser wipes when the tab closes. On a shared lab machine that means your details are never left behind.

REST APIs

Content that updates itself

The AI News page queries the Hacker News API and the Projects page queries the GitHub API, both through jQuery $.ajax(). Nobody on the committee edits those sections by hand.

Front end

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript

One stylesheet drives all nine pages, built on a fluid grid with CSS custom properties for theming. Every illustration is hand-written SVG rather than stock photography.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your browser

There is no server and no analytics. Everything described above is stored on your own device, and you can inspect or delete all of it at any time.
