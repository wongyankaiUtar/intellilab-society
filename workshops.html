How a session runs

Two hours, one working thing

1

Setup (15 min)

Everything is pre-installed on lab machines, or one link for your own laptop. Nobody loses an hour to environment problems.

2

Walkthrough (45 min)

The host builds it live and gets stuck on purpose, because debugging is the part textbooks skip.

3

Your turn (45 min)

You extend it with your own data or idea. Committee members circulate to unblock you.

4

Share (15 min)

Three volunteers demo what broke and what worked. The best ones become Projects page entries.
